SPONSORSHIP

We, the team of AIOUS, are excited to invite you to partner with us and put your company in the spotlight at Formula Bharat and Supra SAE. These prestigious national events attract over 2500 engineering students from universities across the country, as well as numerous motorsport enthusiasts. By participating, your brand will gain tremendous visibility and demonstrate a strong commitment to nurturing the talent and potential of budding engineers.

Our mission is to empower women in engineering, and we believe your involvement can make a significant impact. By aligning with us, you will not only promote your products among the participating teams but also showcase your dedication to gender diversity in engineering. Your support will help us highlight our capabilities and foster an environment where women excel.

Moreover, we are dedicated to supporting you in promoting any recently launched products. We will ensure that your brand and products receive extensive publicity at the events and continue to be highlighted within and beyond our college even after the event concludes.

Join us in this incredible journey to empower the next generation of engineers and gain unparalleled publicity opportunities. Let's work together to create a lasting impact and demonstrate our shared commitment to innovation and gender diversity in engineering.

SPONSORSHIP CATEGORIES

FINANCIAL/ MONETARY SPONSORS

Since Formula bharat is capital- intensive projects, we welcome monetary sponsorship which will enable us to purchase material, tools and equipment.

TRAVEL SPONSORS

As the Official Travel Sponsor, your company will support the travel expenses of the team and the vehicle from college to the main event site.

PARTS AND MATERIAL SPONSORS

Our design often consists of standardized parts (wheels, steering, brake calipers etc.). Any part or material sponsorship will be beneficial to the project.

APPAREL AND FOOTWEAR SPONSORS

As the Official Apparel and Footwear sponsor, you will provide the team with its branded apparel and footwear for both formal occasions and other dignitaries.

MEDIA SPONSORS

As the Official Media Sponsor, your company will support the media related efforts (print, electronic and internet media) of the team and the vehicle from the inception of the competition through to the virtual and main events and beyond.

PARTNERSHIP SPONSORSHIP

Involves forming strategic alliances between organizations to support common goals or initiatives. This type of sponsorship often involves a mutually beneficial relationship where both parties contribute resources, expertise, or other support.

ADVISORY SPONSORSHIP

Although less common, monitoring sponsorship involves providing oversight or surveillance support to the sponsored entity. This can include monitoring compliance with regulations, tracking performance metrics, or ensuring the proper use of resources.
