About Us

Aious Formula Student is a team of Mechanical and Automation Engineering students with a shared passion for motorsports and building machines that move. Every year, we design and build a combustion-style Formula race car, applying classroom concepts to real-world challenges. It’s where we learn together, solve problems as a team and grow through hands-on engineering.

Proudly representing IGDTUW, we’re preparing for Formula Bharat 2026 with fresh energy and focus. Since 2011, AIOUS has been more than just a racing team, it’s a space to learn, grow and build lasting collaborations around engineering and sustainable mobility.

Aim

Precision Engineering, Peak Performance

We design a sleek, compact, highperformance single-seat racing car that prioritizes simplicity, comfort, and safety. Adhering to competition standards, we refine every detail, building on past successes to push innovation. With each iteration, we strive for efficiency, safety, and a competitive edge on the track

Vision

Aious Formula Student: Innovating the Future

At Aious Formula Student, our vision is to create a space where innovation meets inclusivity. We are here to do more than just build race cars , we are building a culture that values creativity, collaboration and confidence. In a field often dominated by stereotypes, we aim to lead with skill and passion, proving that engineering and motorsports are for everyone. With each design, test and race, we are pushing boundaries, not just of speed, but of what a student team can achieve. We hope to leave behind a legacy rooted in engineering excellence, meaningful teamwork and the courage to challenge the norm.

ABOUT IGDTUW

Indira Gandhi Delhi Technical University for Women (IGDTUW) stands as a pioneer in technical education, being India’s first and only women’s technical university. Established by the Government of Delhi, it symbolizes a commitment to empowerment, innovation, and excellence.

With a strong focus on research, entrepreneurship, and interdisciplinary learning, IGDTUW nurtures women engineers who are shaping industries and driving change worldwide. Its students and alumni continue to excel in global competitions, startups, and leadership roles, making IGDTUW a hub of innovation and inspiration.

As the home of Team Aious, IGDTUW provides constant support, encouragement, and resources that enable us to compete on national and international platforms, proudly representing the spirit of the university.
