DEPARTMENTS

Chassis

The Chassis Department designs and maintains the vehicle’s main frame, ensuring it’s strong, safe, and well-integrated with the powertrain and suspension for optimal performance and durability.

Vehicle Dynamics & Suspension

The Suspension Team designs and develops systems that ensure the vehicle’s stability, handling, and comfort, keeping it balanced across terrains and during high-speed runs.

Powertrain

The department handles the engine and its integration, optimizing performance, efficiency, and reliability while ensuring it meets overall design goals for on-track performance.

Suspension

The suspension system ensures vehicle stability, handling, and comfort. It’s designed to keep the car balanced under all conditions, fine-tuning performance for smooth rides and precise control on the track.

Engine

The engine system handles power generation and its integration into the vehicle, focusing on performance, efficiency, and reliability to ensure optimal on-track performance.

Brakes

The braking system ensures driver safety and vehicle control, providing reliable, balanced stopping power while meeting performance and event regulations.

ABOUT IGDTUW

Indira Gandhi Delhi Technical University for Women (IGDTUW) stands as a pioneer in technical education, being India's first and only women's technical university. Established by the Government of Delhi, it symbolizes a commitment to empowerment, innovation, and excellence.

With a strong focus on research, entrepreneurship, and interdisciplinary learning, IGDTUW nurtures women engineers who are shaping industries and driving change worldwide. Its students and alumni continue to excel in global competitions, startups, and leadership roles, making IGDTUW a hub of innovation and inspiration.

As the home of TEAM AIOUS, IGDTUW provides constant support, encouragement, and resources that enable us to compete on national and international platforms, proudly representing the spirit of the university.
