OUR JOURNEY

Founded in 2011 at IGDTUW, Team AIOUS is India’s first all-girls Formula Student team with a mission to break barriers in motorsport and engineering. Since its inception, the team has consistently grown in strength, innovation, and performance, from building our very first prototype to proudly competing at national and international levels. Over the years, AIOUS has become a symbol of determination, teamwork, and women’s empowerment in engineering.
