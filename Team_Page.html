MEET THE TEAM

Team Aious is the official all-women Formula Student team of Indira Gandhi Delhi Technical University for Women (IGDTUW). Originally founded in 2007, the team was revived in 2024 with 10 members and a renewed vision to bring hands-on motorsport engineering back to campus.
Now a strong team of 22 members from Mechanical and Automation Engineering, we design, build, and test a combustion-style Formula race car completely in-house. Our work involves applying core engineering concepts to real-world systems — from chassis and aerodynamics to power and performance.
